Research Projects

My research explores how we can design AI systems that are interpretable, collaborative, and aligned with human values. Each project represents an attempt to bridge the gap between technical capability and human understanding.

Current Projects

Explainable AI for Healthcare

NSF Grant • $1.2M • 2023-2026

Developing interpretable machine learning models for medical diagnosis that provide clear reasoning to healthcare professionals. The project involves creating visual explanation interfaces, conducting user studies with clinicians, and validating interpretability across different medical domains including radiology and pathology.

Python, PyTorch, React, clinical datasets

Interactive Model Debugging

Industry Partnership • Google Research

Creating tools that allow developers to interactively explore and debug machine learning models. The system enables real-time visualization of model behavior, identification of biases, and collaborative debugging sessions between technical and non-technical stakeholders.

TensorFlow, D3.js, WebGL, graph neural networks

Human-in-the-Loop Learning

Stanford HAI • Ongoing

Investigating how human feedback can be incorporated throughout the machine learning pipeline, from data collection to model training to deployment. The project focuses on developing algorithms that can learn from sparse human feedback while maintaining efficiency and scalability.

Reinforcement learning, active learning, crowdsourcing platforms

Completed Projects

Visual Attention in Deep Networks

PhD Dissertation • 2021

Comprehensive study of attention mechanisms in computer vision models, exploring how visual attention patterns can be made interpretable to humans. The work resulted in new visualization techniques and evaluation metrics for attention-based explanations.

Computer vision, attention mechanisms, psychophysics experiments

Collaborative AI for Design

Adobe Research Internship • 2020

Developed AI tools that assist graphic designers in creative workflows. The system learned from designer behavior to suggest improvements while maintaining creative control with the human designer. Led to two patent applications and integration into Adobe's creative suite.

Generative models, user interface design, creative AI
