Building the future of human-AI collaboration

I'm an Assistant Professor of Computer Science at Stanford, where I lead the Human-AI Interaction Lab. My research focuses on making AI systems more interpretable and collaborative, bridging the gap between technical capability and human understanding.

I'm also the co-founder of Clarity AI, a startup that helps organizations implement responsible AI practices. We've worked with over 200 companies to audit and improve their machine learning systems.
