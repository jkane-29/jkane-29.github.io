How the positions are made The illustration's route network is out of date and incomplete, so missing corridors (Cascades north of Seattle, the Surfliner to San Diego, the Michigan services, Roanoke, Keystone and others) were reconstructed from real station coordinates and drawn in. Trains are snapped to the nearest point on the combined network within 75 px; anything further out is hidden rather than floated. Via Rail services and any train north of the Canadian border are filtered out — the map only covers the lower 48. Trains are placed with a thin-plate-spline warp fitted to the drawing's coastline, so positions are approximate: the artwork's own geography is stretched (Texas is squat, Florida is long, the West is compressed).
