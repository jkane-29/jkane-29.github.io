Biking the Perimeter of Chicago

September 21, 2025

A journey around the edges of the city, exploring the boundaries where urban meets nature, industry meets residential, and the familiar meets the unknown.

The Route

The perimeter of Chicago spans approximately 28 miles, following the city's official boundaries. Starting from the lakefront in the north, the route winds through diverse neighborhoods, industrial areas, and green spaces that most residents never see.

North Side: Lakefront to Northwest

Beginning at Montrose Harbor, the path follows the lakefront south before turning west through Lincoln Park. The transition from the manicured parkland to the industrial corridors along the Chicago River reveals the city's layered history.

West Side: Industry and Innovation

The western boundary cuts through areas that have shaped Chicago's identity as an industrial powerhouse. Old factories stand alongside new developments, creating a landscape that tells the story of the city's economic evolution.

South Side: Communities and Connections

The southern perimeter passes through neighborhoods that represent the heart of Chicago's cultural diversity. Here, the city's boundaries feel more like suggestions than hard lines, with communities extending beyond official limits.

East Side: Return to the Lake

The final leg follows the lakefront north, completing the circuit. The contrast between the urban density and the vastness of Lake Michigan provides a fitting conclusion to the journey.

Reflections

Biking the perimeter offers a unique perspective on Chicago's scale and complexity. The city reveals itself not as a single entity, but as a collection of distinct areas connected by shared boundaries and common purpose.

"The best way to understand a city is to trace its edges, where it meets the world beyond."

This journey around Chicago's perimeter was more than just a bike ride—it was an exploration of the city's identity, its history, and its place in the larger landscape of the Midwest.
