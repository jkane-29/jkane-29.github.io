Ideas

Random thoughts and half-baked theories across different fields. Some might be worth pursuing, others are probably better left as ideas.

Urban Planning & Transit

Transit-oriented development could paradoxically increase affordability by creating density that supports local businesses, reducing the need for car ownership and freeing up household budgets for housing.

What affect would noise abatement for light rail have on development in surrounding areas?

Regulation & Market Structures

Is there a word for when a good has one buyer? Are we likely to be "overpaying" as the sole purchaser of something, do goods with many buyers have a natural equilibrium that is roughgly a market price?

How does the structure of the long-term care market influence outcomes? Ex. Assisted living is largely private pay, while nurisng is medicaid/medicare.

Have more careers become licensed? Are more young people interested in these careers/is there a non-standard political affiliation.

Is dynamic pricing more effective for a good with elastic or inelastic demand?

Version control for legislation: what if policy changes were tracked, discussed, and merged like code, with clear attribution and reasoning for each modification?

Education & Learning

The best way to teach programming might be through collaborative debugging sessions rather than individual coding exercises.

Academic conferences would be more valuable if they included "failure sessions" where researchers shared what didn't work and why.

Learning retention improves when students have to teach the material to others—what if peer teaching was built into every curriculum from elementary school onwards?
